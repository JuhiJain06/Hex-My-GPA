Incognito

Incognito windows aren't blocked or reported until you allow the extension there:

  1. Open chrome://extensions (or edge://extensions in Edge) in the address bar.
  2. Find Study Buddy and click Details.
  3. Turn on Allow in Incognito.
  4. Open the extension's options page and click Re-check.
